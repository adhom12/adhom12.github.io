Privacy Policy: Weekly Summary Bot

Last updated: 21 October 2026

What it accesses

With the signed-in user's permission, the bot has read-only access to the Google Calendars that user can see. It reads event titles, times and attendee email addresses for the current week.

How the data is used

Events are checked against a fixed list of team members. For matching events, the bot posts the event title, time and the team member's name to the team's private Telegram group. Nothing else is posted or shared.

Storage

Calendar data is processed only while the weekly job runs and is not saved afterwards. The bot never creates, edits or deletes calendar events.

Sharing

Calendar data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The bot's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
